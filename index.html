Why Your Online Presence Matters

Your next customer is probably checking you out right now.

Before someone calls, visits or makes an enquiry, there's a good chance they'll look you up online.

And whether you realise it or not, they're forming an opinion about your business.

Stay Relevant

FAQ

A Few Common Questions.

Why is Facebook marketing important for local businesses?

Facebook is a great way for local businesses to stay connected with their communities, showcase their work and build trust with potential customers.

Consistent, engaging content helps people get to know your business before they need your products or services.

When the time comes to buy, they're more likely to remember a business they've seen regularly and feel familiar with.

What does RC Marketing actually do?

We're a Sydney-based marketing agency specialising in Facebook marketing and social media management for small and medium-sized businesses.

We also help with content creation, branding, website design, SEO, Google reviews and marketing strategy.

Our goal is to make your marketing easier while helping your business become more recognisable and trusted.

Can you manage our Facebook page for us?

Absolutely!

We can help plan, create and publish content for your Facebook business page, keeping your brand active and visible while you focus on running your business.

We take the time to understand your business so your content feels genuine, relevant and consistent with your brand.

Do you visit our business to create content?

Yes! One of our favourite parts of the job is visiting businesses, meeting their teams and capturing the real stories behind their work.

We offer on-site content creation for Sydney-based clients, with visits arranged according to your location and service package.

Do I need a website if I already have Facebook?

Facebook is great for building awareness and staying connected with your customers, while your website gives people a dedicated place to learn more about your services and make an enquiry.

Having both can help strengthen your online presence and make your business easier to discover.

What is an AI-optimised website?

An AI-optimised website is built with clear, useful content and a logical structure that helps search engines and AI-powered discovery tools understand your business.

We combine traditional SEO foundations with modern website practices to improve discoverability and make information easier for customers to find.

No website can guarantee placement in Google or AI-generated recommendations.

Can AI help my small business?

Definitely, when it's used for the right tasks.

AI tools can help businesses with customer enquiries, content planning, routine administration and repetitive processes.

We focus on practical solutions that suit your business rather than adding technology just for the sake of it.

How do we start working with RC Marketing?

We like to keep things simple.

First, we'll organise a friendly discovery call to learn more about your business, goals and what you'd like help with.

If we're a good fit, we'll recommend a practical approach, agree on the scope of work and organise the next steps.

Depending on availability and the service required, we can often get started within a few business days.

How long does Facebook marketing take to work?

Building a strong brand takes consistency.

Some businesses notice improvements in engagement, visibility and enquiries within the first few months, while others take longer depending on their starting point, audience and goals.

We focus on building lasting recognition and trust rather than chasing quick results that don't contribute to your business.

Do you only work with Sydney businesses?

Not at all!

We're proudly based in Sydney and love working with local businesses where we can visit in person, capture content and build strong working relationships.

We've also worked with businesses across Australia and can support clients remotely depending on their needs.

What types of businesses do you work with?

We've worked with businesses across automotive, trades, construction, hospitality, tourism, retail and professional services.

We particularly enjoy partnering with small and medium-sized businesses that take pride in what they do and want more people to know about it.
