FAQ

A Few Common Questions.

How can Facebook marketing help my small business grow?

Consistent Facebook marketing helps local businesses increase visibility, build customer trust and stay front of mind when people are ready to buy. Sharing genuine stories, customer experiences and useful content can also encourage enquiries, referrals and long-term brand recognition.

What does RC Marketing's Facebook management service include?

We manage your Facebook business page through content planning, post creation, publishing, organic growth strategies and community engagement. Facebook ad management can be added on as part of the same service. Think of us as your in-house marketing team, keeping your business active, professional and connected with local customers.

Do you visit our business to create social media content?

Absolutely! We regularly visit clients across Sydney's Hills District and North Shore to capture authentic photos, videos, team stories and behind-the-scenes content, with visits arranged around your service package.

How much does Facebook marketing cost for a small business?

Our Facebook marketing, ad management and social media packages are tailored to your business goals, content requirements and level of support. Get in touch for a friendly discovery call, and we'll recommend an approach that suits your business.

How long does Facebook marketing take to generate results?

Some businesses notice improvements in engagement, visibility and customer enquiries within the first few months, although results vary. We focus on consistent, long-term brand growth rather than promising overnight success.

Do I need a website if my business already has Facebook?

Facebook helps customers discover your business and build familiarity, while a professional website gives them a dedicated place to explore your services and make enquiries. Together, they can strengthen your online presence and help potential customers find and trust your business.

Where does RC Marketing work, and what businesses do you help?

We're a Sydney-based marketing agency working closely with furniture retailers, trades and service businesses across the Hills District, North Shore and surrounding suburbs. We also support businesses across Australia remotely, with services including Facebook marketing and ads, content creation, branding, website design and SEO.
